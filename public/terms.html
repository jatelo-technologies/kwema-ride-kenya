Kwema Ride Kenya — riders and drivers

1. The service

Kwema Ride Kenya connects riders with independent drivers. Kwema Ride Kenya is not itself a carrier; the driver provides the transport and Kwema Ride Kenya provides the platform.

2. Your account

You must be 18 or older and provide a valid phone number. You are responsible for activity on your account. Never share your verification code.

3. Price and payment

The price shown before you request is the price you pay, unless the route changes or there is extended waiting, which may add charges shown in the app. You may pay by cash, M-Pesa or Airtel Money, or by card where available. Cancellation fees may apply after a driver accepts.

4. Conduct

  • Respect the driver, the vehicle and other road users.
  • No illegal or dangerous goods.
  • Drivers must hold a valid driving licence, transport licence, insurance and a roadworthy vehicle, and obey traffic law.
  • Kwema Ride Kenya may suspend an account for fraud, abuse or danger.

5. Safety

The SOS button alerts the operator and your emergency contact; it does not replace the emergency services. In danger, call the official emergency numbers immediately.

6. Parcels and food

The delivery service carries parcels and orders handed over by a third party. The sender warrants that the contents are lawful. The handover code is proof of delivery.

7. Liability

To the extent the law allows, Kwema Ride Kenya is not liable for indirect loss, and its total liability to you is limited to the amount you paid for the trip concerned. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by Kenyan law. Disputes belong to the courts of Nairobi unless the law requires otherwise. [To be confirmed by legal counsel.]

Kwema Ride Kenya — abiria na madereva

1. Huduma

Kwema Ride Kenya inaunganisha abiria na madereva huru. Kwema Ride Kenya yenyewe si msafirishaji; dereva hutoa usafiri na Kwema Ride Kenya hutoa jukwaa.

2. Akaunti yako

Lazima uwe na umri wa miaka 18 au zaidi na utoe namba halali ya simu. Unawajibika kwa shughuli zote kwenye akaunti yako. Usishiriki msimbo wako wa uthibitisho.

3. Bei na malipo

Bei inayoonyeshwa kabla ya kuomba ndiyo unayolipa, isipokuwa njia ikibadilika au ukisubiri kwa muda mrefu, jambo linaloweza kuongeza gharama zitakazoonyeshwa kwenye app. Unaweza kulipa kwa pesa taslimu, M-Pesa au Airtel Money, au kwa kadi inapopatikana. Ada ya kughairi inaweza kutumika baada ya dereva kukubali.

4. Mwenendo

  • Heshimu dereva, chombo na watumiaji wengine wa barabara.
  • Hakuna bidhaa haramu au hatari.
  • Madereva lazima wawe na leseni halali ya udereva, leseni ya usafirishaji, bima na chombo chenye ubora, na wafuate sheria za barabarani.
  • Kwema Ride Kenya inaweza kusimamisha akaunti kwa ulaghai, unyanyasaji au hatari.

5. Usalama

Kitufe cha SOS kinaarifu mwendeshaji na mtu wako wa dharura; hakichukui nafasi ya huduma za dharura. Ukiwa hatarini, piga namba rasmi za dharura mara moja.

6. Vifurushi na chakula

Huduma ya uwasilishaji hubeba vifurushi na oda zinazokabidhiwa na mtu wa tatu. Mtumaji anathibitisha kuwa yaliyomo ni halali. Msimbo wa kukabidhi ni uthibitisho wa kufikishwa.

7. Dhima

Kwa kiwango ambacho sheria inaruhusu, Kwema Ride Kenya haihusiki na hasara zisizo za moja kwa moja, na dhima yake yote kwako ni kiasi ulicholipa kwa safari husika. Hakuna kilichomo hapa kinachoondoa dhima isiyoweza kuondolewa kisheria.

8. Sheria inayotumika

Masharti haya yanasimamiwa na sheria za Kenya. Migogoro itasikilizwa na mahakama za Nairobi isipokuwa sheria inahitaji vinginevyo. [Itathibitishwa na mshauri wa kisheria.]
