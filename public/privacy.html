Kwema Ride Kenya — riders and drivers

1. Who we are

Kwema Ride Kenya is a service that connects riders and drivers (rides, parcels, food), operated by [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME], [address], Nairobi, Kenya (“we”). Contact: support@kwemaride.co.ke.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: phone number, name, preferred language, optional profile photo.
  • Drivers: driving licence, transport licence, insurance, identity card, photo, vehicle details.
  • Trips: pickup and drop-off points, route, time, price, ratings, and messages exchanged with the other person on the trip.
  • Location: the device’s position while you use the app and, for online drivers, in the background.
  • Payment: mobile wallet operator and number, transaction reference. We never see or store card numbers: they are entered on the payment provider’s secure page.
  • Safety: SOS alerts and the emergency contact you choose.
  • Technical: device identifier, notification token, error logs.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the service: match you, calculate the price, process payment.
  • For your safety: SOS alerts, driver verification, fraud prevention.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations that apply to transport operations.
  • To improve and troubleshoot the service. We do not sell your data.

4. What the other person sees

The driver sees your first name, photo, and your pickup and drop-off points. You see the driver’s name, photo, rating and vehicle. The other person’s phone number is shared only during a live trip.

5. Sharing

We share data with: payment operators (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, our card provider) to process your payments; our technical providers (hosting, maps, SMS, notifications); and authorities where the law requires it. Each provider is bound to protect the data.

6. Retention

We keep trip data as long as accounting, safety and the law require, then delete or anonymise it. You may ask us to delete your account at any time.

7. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and withdraw location permission in your phone settings. Write to support@kwemaride.co.ke. [Registration with the data protection authority: reference to be added.]

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted, verification codes expire quickly, and administrator access is restricted and logged. No system is perfect: report any concern to the address above.

We will post material changes here. Last updated: [DATE].

Kwema Ride Kenya — abiria na madereva

1. Sisi ni nani

Kwema Ride Kenya ni huduma inayounganisha abiria na madereva (safari, vifurushi, chakula), inayoendeshwa na [JINA RASMI LA MWENDESHAJI], [anwani], Nairobi, Kenya (“sisi”). Mawasiliano: support@kwemaride.co.ke.

2. Data tunayokusanya

  • Akaunti: namba ya simu, jina, lugha unayopendelea, picha ya wasifu (hiari).
  • Madereva: leseni ya udereva, leseni ya usafirishaji, bima, kitambulisho, picha, taarifa za chombo.
  • Safari: pa kukuchukua na pa kukushusha, njia, muda, bei, ukadiriaji, na ujumbe kati yako na mtu mwingine kwenye safari.
  • Mahali: mahali pa kifaa chako unapotumia app na, kwa madereva walio mtandaoni, hata chinichini.
  • Malipo: mtoa huduma wa pochi ya simu na namba, rejea ya muamala. Hatuoni wala kuhifadhi namba za kadi: zinaingizwa kwenye ukurasa salama wa mtoa huduma wa malipo.
  • Usalama: tahadhari za SOS na mtu wa dharura uliyemchagua.
  • Kiufundi: kitambulisho cha kifaa, tokeni ya arifa, kumbukumbu za hitilafu.

3. Kwa nini tunaitumia

  • Kutoa huduma: kukulinganisha na dereva, kukokotoa bei, kushughulikia malipo.
  • Kwa usalama wako: tahadhari za SOS, uthibitishaji wa madereva, kuzuia ulaghai.
  • Kutimiza wajibu wa kisheria na kisimamizi unaohusu usafirishaji.
  • Kuboresha huduma na kutatua matatizo. Hatuuzi data yako.

4. Mtu mwingine anaona nini

Dereva anaona jina lako la kwanza, picha, na pa kukuchukua na pa kukushusha. Wewe unaona jina la dereva, picha, ukadiriaji na chombo. Namba ya simu ya mtu mwingine inashirikiwa tu wakati safari inaendelea.

5. Kushiriki

Tunashiriki data na: watoa huduma za malipo (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, mtoa huduma wa kadi) ili kushughulikia malipo yako; watoa huduma wetu wa kiufundi (mwenyeji wa mfumo, ramani, SMS, arifa); na mamlaka pale sheria inapotaka. Kila mtoa huduma analazimika kulinda data.

6. Muda wa kuhifadhi

Tunahifadhi data ya safari kwa muda ambao uhasibu, usalama na sheria vinahitaji, kisha tunaifuta au kuifanya isitambulike. Unaweza kuomba tufute akaunti yako wakati wowote.

7. Haki zako

Chini ya Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, unaweza kuomba kupata, kusahihisha au kufuta data yako, na kuondoa ruhusa ya mahali kwenye mipangilio ya simu. Andika kwa support@kwemaride.co.ke. [Usajili na mamlaka ya ulinzi wa data: rejea itaongezwa.]
